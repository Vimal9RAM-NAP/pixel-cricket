🎮 HOW TO PLAY
• Desktop: Arrow Left/Right to position batsman. Spacebar to Swing.
• Mobile: Touch & drag left/right on pitch to position batsman. Tap pitch to Swing.
• Loft/Ground: Use Arrow Up/Down or tap the screen buttons.
• Power Shot: Fills automatically! When meter reaches 100%, next Spacebar / Tap releases a Power Shot!
